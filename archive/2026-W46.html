Technology Digest

Week 2026-W46

Management summary

  • Copilot and Copilot-adjacent governance changes are coming fast: EU data boundary enablement for Claude models (MC1546438), DLP controls to restrict Copilot grounding on external/untrusted emails (MC1301714), and retirement/migration items affecting Copilot experiences in Power BI (MC1546429) and MODS Workforce Insights ingestion endpoints (MC1542066).
  • Power Platform governance and admin visibility are improving: a new Connector inventory capability in the Power Platform admin center (MC1546312) and a new default policy for feedback form checkbox settings (MC1545686).
  • Copilot Cowork’s connector behavior and Planner integration are changing: Power Platform connectors follow Microsoft 365 admin center settings (MC1542062), and the Planner Plugin enables Planner task actions with admin controls and user approval before updates (MC1540339).
  • Security and compliance updates include Defender for Office 365 recipient inclusion logic changes (MC1546447), a new Defender remediation action to move suspicious emails to quarantine (MC1523084), and Purview DLP setup improvements for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business (MC1489971).
  • Several Teams changes may affect user experience and security posture: notification pause (MC1465768), malicious QR detection with blurred external QR images (MC1526651), Teams Free off-network invitation retirement (MC1546409), and Teams meeting recording/transcription options aligning to admin policies (MC1508234).
  • Dynamics 365-related operational controls are being tightened: Dynamics 365 Contact Center adds role-based enforcement for recording/transcript downloads (MC1485169).

Calls to action

  • Validate Copilot governance impacts for EU tenants and grounding controls — Claude models will be enabled by default for eligible EU/EFTA tenants unless opted out/limited (MC1546438), and Purview DLP will add an admin-configurable control to prevent Copilot from using external/untrusted-domain emails as grounding data (MC1301714).
  • Plan for Power BI Copilot experience retirement and MODS endpoint migration — Standalone Power BI Copilot preview is being retired and Microsoft 365 Copilot in Power BI will be enabled by default while standalone navigation/settings are removed (MC1546429). Also, Workforce Insights custom organizational data ingestion must move from the Workforce Insights endpoint to the Microsoft 365 profile endpoint by November 18, 2026 (MC1542066).
  • Review Power Platform governance workflows using connector inventory and Cowork connector behavior — Connector inventory adds a Connectors column and exposes the same data via admin APIs/CLI/Azure Resource Graph to support governance workflows (MC1546312). Copilot Cowork will support Power Platform connectors whose availability is managed via the Microsoft 365 admin center, and Power Platform admin center DLP policies do not apply to Cowork (MC1542062).
  • Update security/compliance playbooks for Defender and Purview changes — Defender for Office 365 threat protection policies will change recipient inclusion evaluation from AND to OR when multiple recipient types are specified (MC1546447), and Plan 2 introduces a Move to Quarantine remediation action that requires playbook/permissions review (MC1523084). Purview DLP setup/activation for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business is improving with Intune multi-admin approval support (MC1489971).
  • Assess Teams user-impact items and align meeting policy expectations — Teams will add user-controlled notification pausing (MC1465768), malicious QR detection with blurred external QR images (MC1526651), and will retire off-network invitations for Teams Free accounts (MC1546409). Meeting recording/transcription options will align with organizer-assigned Teams meeting policies, including a new “Record only” option (MC1508234).
