Technology Digest

Week 2026-W45

Management summary

  • Copilot and AI governance changes are rolling out across Microsoft 365, Copilot Chat, Copilot app UX, and Copilot Studio—several items affect licensing/credits, fair-use limits, and policy names that may require admin/script updates.
  • Microsoft Defender RBAC and Defender for Office 365 Unified RBAC permission mappings are changing via auto-enable and role mapping updates, with potential admin permission rework for security operators.
  • Power Platform and Power Apps are seeing model and feature retirements (GPT model replacements; canvas app Copilot formula assistance/proactive rename retirement), which may change maker experience and outputs.
  • Dynamics 365 Project Operations has roadmap items for larger invoices and additional budget/baseline capabilities, but the provided text lacks concrete technical details and release state.
  • Teams and Planner experience updates are broad and mostly low-touch (new UI/analytics, new policies/controls, and Planner storage changes), with a few security-relevant items (QR code protection, external bot blocking, facilitator protection).
  • Purview enhancements and DLP integrations are continuing (auto-labeling scalability/reporting, DLP integration with Prisma Access), plus one Content Explorer change was canceled (no action required).
  • Entra ID authentication changes are planned (retire Microsoft-provided SMS/voice for SSPR starting Feb 2027), which may require recovery-method validation to avoid helpdesk disruption.

Calls to action

  • Validate Copilot licensing/credits, fair-use limits, and policy/script impacts — Several Copilot items introduce usage-based billing/credits, fair-use limits for non-licensed users, and new/renamed policies that may require admin or automation updates.
  • Review Defender RBAC/Unified RBAC role mappings and plan permission adjustments — Defender RBAC is auto-enabled and Defender for Office 365 Unified RBAC mapping changes may remove default access, requiring manual permission assignment to keep security operator workflows working.
  • Test and update Power Platform/Power Apps maker experiences impacted by model and feature retirements — GPT model availability changes and canvas app Copilot assistance retirements can alter prompt/response behavior and remove maker features after Dec 11, 2026.
  • Prepare for Entra ID SSPR authentication retirement by validating alternate recovery methods — Microsoft-provided SMS/voice authentication for SSPR is scheduled to retire starting Feb 2027, with a transition period; organizations should validate SSPR and Conditional Access behavior to prevent helpdesk disruption.
  • Confirm Teams security and meeting policy changes that may require admin configuration or documentation updates — Multiple Teams items introduce new security controls and policy options (some optional/off by default, some require policy parameters), and user guidance may need updates.
